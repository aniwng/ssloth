Top 5 Backpacks for Back to School in 2026

updated jul 2026 · 5 min read

A backpack has to survive a full school year of being dropped, overstuffed, and dragged across campus — the bag that looks best on day one isn't always the one still holding together in May.

Every pick here is checked against reviews and testing data from sources like NBC Select, CNN Underscored, and Reviewed before it makes the list. No rush, just really good picks.

1
editor's pick
The North Face Borealis
best dual-purpose backpack
buy now

This one holds up to both classroom routines and weekend hikes, with a stand-up shape, a padded laptop sleeve, and a removable waist strap for longer treks. Owners several years in report it's still holding together, which says more than any first-week impression can.

A 32-liter main compartment and a dedicated organizer panel make this an easy fit for anyone who'd rather not think about where things go. It's been a reliable, unglamorous default for decades, and it still is.

best for a grown-up look

Water-repellent fabric and a clean, unbranded-looking shape mean this one transitions from high school hallways to an internship without looking out of place either way. Multiple interior pockets keep it organized without the bulk of a bigger bag.

5
Matein Extra Large Backpack
best budget oversized pick
buy now

An affordable option that still manages padded, shoulder-friendly straps and enough room for a stack of textbooks and binders. It won't win style points, but it's a dependable choice for anyone who needs the space more than the branding.

quick buying tips

A padded laptop sleeve is worth prioritizing if a device is coming along every day. Beyond that, wide, padded shoulder straps matter more over a full school year than extra pockets do — a bag carried daily should be judged on comfort first. Most day-to-day school needs fit in 20 to 32 liters; anything bigger starts to feel like a travel pack.
