Top 5 Home Tech Gadgets Worth Having in 2026

updated jul 2026 · 5 min read

Most "smart home" advice still assumes you want to automate an entire house at once. The gadgets that actually earn their keep tend to be smaller than that — one device that fixes one specific annoyance, set up in an afternoon and then forgotten about.

Every pick here is checked against reviews and testing data from sources like Engadget, Forbes Vetted, and TechRadar before it makes the list. No rush, just really good picks.

1
editor's pick
Amazon Echo Show 5 (3rd Gen)
best all-around smart home hub
buy now

This one doubles as a home command center: check a video doorbell's feed, run through the day's calendar, and control lights or a thermostat by voice, all from a screen small enough not to dominate a kitchen counter. It plays well with most other ecosystems, which makes it an easy default for a first smart display.

A second camera angled down at the doorstep means it actually catches what's happening at ground level, not just faces at eye height — useful for anyone tired of missing porch deliveries in the frame entirely. Local storage options also mean it can run without a mandatory subscription.

buy now

No smart bulbs or rewiring required — this one turns a regular lamp or fan into something schedulable and voice-controlled in about five minutes. Built-in energy monitoring is a nice bonus for anyone curious what's actually driving up the electric bill.

4

most fun for the size
buy now

A soda-can-sized laser projector that throws a genuinely sharp 100-inch image and runs its own smart TV software, no streaming stick required. Battery life covers a full movie with no cord in sight, which is most of the appeal.

5
Govee M1 RGBIC LED Strip Light
best ambiance upgrade
buy now

Individually addressable color segments mean it can shift through multiple colors at once instead of washing a room in one flat hue, and it can sync to music or whatever's on screen. Setup is stick-and-plug simple, with no separate hub required.

quick buying tips

Check for Matter support before buying anything new — it's become the standard that lets devices from different brands work together without separate apps for each one. Beyond that, start with one or two gadgets that solve an actual annoyance rather than trying to automate the whole house at once; it's a much easier way to tell what's actually worth keeping.
