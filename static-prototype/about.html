about us

Slow picks. Fast results.

Here's the thing about sloths: they move at about 0.15 miles per hour. They sleep up to 15 hours a day. They will not be rushed.

But sloths are also incredibly deliberate. Every move they make is efficient, necessary, and considered. If a sloth is going somewhere, it's going somewhere good. That's basically our whole editorial philosophy.

Why a sloth?

We got tired of "top 10" lists clearly written in twenty minutes by someone who never read past the first review. So we decided to be the opposite: slow, thorough, and mildly suspicious of anything claiming to be "revolutionary."

What we actually do

Every pick is checked against reviews and testing data from sources that actually put products through the wringer. We take our time before telling you what's worth buying.

A note on affiliate links

Some of our links are affiliate links, and we may earn a small commission if you buy through them, at no extra cost to you. Commissions never influence our picks.

"We move slow, but only toward the good stuff."

— the shopping sloth team
